Basilisk puts a Sony E lens on your astro camera and drives its focus and aperture from the warm
room. The glass bends to your will; the cold stays outside.

![Basilisk on the bench, under a green moon](images/basilisk-on-the-bench.png)

## What is in the box

- one adapter board, bare: you solder it, then check it (see [Usage](usage.en.md));
- the [schematics](../../1_Board/Basilisk-v1.0b_1-Schematics.pdf), straight from the repository;
- a firmware, installed from the Setup tab.
